How it works:
The calculator finds the optimal points to use so that you earn back equal or more points than you spend, maximizing your rewards efficiency.

💡 Understanding the Optimization

Points Earning

Points are calculated based on your card's reward structure. The amount is always rounded down to the nearest spend threshold.

Optimal Usage

The optimizer finds the perfect balance where you use points to reduce the price, but still earn back the same or more points from the purchase.

Example

For a ₹6,000 item with a card earning 25 points per ₹150: Use 850 points to pay ₹5,150, and earn back exactly 850 points. Perfect optimization! 🎯
